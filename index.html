Version History

v2.3.1

Offline mode no longer breaks if an icon file is missing.

v2.3.0

Refreshed app icon with a proper loaf shape and crust shading, plus sharp PNG icons for iPhone and Android home screens and notifications.

v2.2.1

Animations are about 30% less bouncy, and the stray blue square around the current step is gone.

v2.2.0

The step you're on now animates through stages (dough rising, starter filling the jar, the oven glowing…) inside a ring that fills as the step goes on.

v2.1.0

Bouncy, springy animations throughout the app, and End bake now asks for a second tap instead of a browser dialog (which could be silently blocked).

v2.0.0

New Timeline tab: plans your bake around a ready time or starts now, keeps steps out of your sleep hours, lets you customize step times, and sends gentle reminders while you bake.

v1.9.0

Replaced emojis with a custom hand-drawn icon set, including bread-style icons and new tab icons.

v1.8.0

Works fully offline, remembers your inputs and theme, warns on impossible starter/hydration combos, and adds oil & sugar to Custom Recipe.

v1.7.0

Split app into proper PWA files (manifest, sw.js) to trigger native Android install prompt.

v1.6.0

Updated salt, oil, and sugar outputs to show one decimal place.

v1.5.0

Added custom vector app icon.
